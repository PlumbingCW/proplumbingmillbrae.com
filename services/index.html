Plumbing problems rarely announce which category they belong to. A wet spot on the ceiling might be a supply leak, a drain issue or a failing water heater. The groups below are organized the way a dispatcher thinks: how urgent is it, and where is the water going?

If water is flowing and you cannot stop it, close the main shut-off first, then call. Everything else on this page can wait a few minutes while you read.

Emergency repairs when water will not stop

These are the services where hours matter. Active leaks, split pipes and failing lines damage floors, drywall and belongings quickly, and the 24/7 line exists for them.

Burst pipe repair

Cracked or split pipes, repaired fast so the water can be turned back on.

Leak detection

Locating hidden leaks in walls, ceilings and crawlspaces without guesswork.

Slab leak repair

Pressurized lines under a concrete floor, found and fixed.

Water service line repair

The buried line from the meter to your house, repaired or replaced.

Commercial plumbing

Emergency leaks, grease traps and backflow testing for businesses.

Hot water, from a cold shower to a planned upgrade

A water heater usually fails on its own schedule, which is rarely convenient. We repair what can be repaired, replace what cannot, and install new units for homeowners who plan ahead.

Water heater repair

No hot water, leaks and lighting problems diagnosed on site.

Water heater replacement

A failed tank removed and a new unit set in its place.

Water heater installation

Planned installs, including gas, electric and heat pump options.

Tankless water heater

On-demand units installed, repaired and descaled.

Drains, mainlines and sewers

Most drain calls start small and become urgent when a second fixture starts gurgling. In Millbrae the homeowner is responsible for the sewer lateral on the property, so the line from your house to the main is worth understanding.

Drain cleaning

Sinks, tubs, showers and branch lines cleared.

Hydro jetting

High-pressure water that scours the inside of the pipe.

Sewer line cleaning

Main lines cleared when the whole house backs up.

Sewer camera inspection

A camera shows the cause and the condition of the line.

Sewer line repair

Spot repair, trenchless lining and replacement.

Fixtures, pipes and installs

Toilet repair and installation

Faucet repair

Garbage disposal

Repiping

Plumbing installation

Fixtures, remodel plumbing and new lines.

Water quality and high bills

Millbrae water comes from the San Francisco Regional Water System and is treated with chloramine. The city's 2025 water quality report lists average hardness at 62 ppm, which is soft by common scales. That shapes what a softener or filter can and cannot do for you, and we will say so plainly.

Water softener and filtration

Treatment systems matched to the problem you are trying to solve.

High water bill and meter leaks

Finding the leak behind a bill that does not make sense.

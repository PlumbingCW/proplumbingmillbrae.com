Most plumbing questions come up at awkward moments: a toilet that keeps running, a water heater making a new noise, a filter you are not sure you need. The guides below are written for those moments. Each one explains the problem in ordinary words, shows what is safe to try yourself, and says where a plumber earns the call.

We write them around how plumbing actually works in Millbrae. The city's water comes from the San Francisco Regional Water System, many homes date from the postwar building boom, and local sewer rules put the lateral on the homeowner. Those facts change what advice is sensible, so the guides use them where they apply.

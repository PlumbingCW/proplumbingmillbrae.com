Trenchless Plumbing Repair in Millbrae: Lining, Bursting and When to Dig

Trenchless repair fixes a sewer lateral from a couple of small access points instead of opening a long trench across your yard or driveway.

It is not right for every pipe, and in Millbrae the city sewer code shapes the decision more than most homeowners expect.

Trenchless repair is a family of methods that replace or rehabilitate an underground pipe without digging its whole length. The two you hear about most are cured-in-place lining and pipe bursting. Both work through small pits at the ends of the run, which is a big deal when the pipe passes under a driveway, a patio, mature landscaping or the sidewalk.

That said, trenchless is a tool, not a promise. Some pipes cannot be lined, and some failures need a spot dig anyway. The first job is to learn what the pipe really looks like inside.

Start with the camera, not the method

Nobody can pick a repair method from the symptom alone. A slow drain, a gurgle in a floor drain or roots reappearing every spring all point to a lateral problem, but not to which kind. A camera inspection records the pipe from the cleanout toward the main and shows cracks, offset joints, roots, belly sections that hold water and any collapse.

The recording also gives you something concrete to compare quotes against. If one company says the whole lateral needs replacing and another says a section will do, the footage is what settles it. Our sewer camera inspection service is built around exactly that step.

How the two main methods work

Cured-in-place lining

A flexible resin-soaked liner is pulled into the existing pipe, inflated against the walls and cured so it hardens into a new pipe inside the old one. It suits pipes that are still in line but cracked, leaking at joints or rough from age. It will not fix a collapsed section or a deep belly that holds water, because the liner follows whatever shape is already there.

Pipe bursting

A bursting head is pulled through the old pipe, breaking it outward while a new pipe is drawn in behind it. It can restore full diameter and even upsize slightly, and it handles pipes that are too far gone to line. The tradeoff is that it needs entry and exit pits, and nearby utilities and the surrounding soil have to be understood first.

Local rules that change the plan

What the Millbrae sewer code asks of you

Under Chapter 8.20 of the Millbrae Municipal Code, the owner builds, maintains and clears the sewer lateral on their own lot. The city maintains only the part between the cleanout wye and the main, and only when the cleanout is in the designated location. So a root problem inside your property line is yours to fix, and the code gives you 120 days after notice to remove roots and repair the pipe to prevent a repeat.

Chapter 8.20 also ties lateral work to permits. A building permit that adds plumbing fixtures or enlarges more than 25 percent of a building calls for a camera inspection by a licensed plumber or sewer contractor first, and a lateral that fails must be repaired within 90 days. The code lists a backwater valve and relief valve among the items for qualifying work, so plan for them when you price a lateral job.

For buyers and sellers, the code also describes lateral testing at the time of sale for buildings more than 20 years old. The exact enforcement status depends on the city's current program, so call Public Works before assuming it applies or does not. If a camera inspection shows a lateral failing, the repair method you choose should be one the city will accept.

When trenchless is a poor idea

Trenchless methods struggle where the pipe has fallen apart, where the slope is wrong or where a large belly collects waste. Lining over a belly just creates a nicely coated trap. A pipe with several sharp offsets can be difficult to burst or line without cutting access points along the route.

There is also a practical issue with ground conditions. Millbrae's general plan notes that the San Andreas Fault lies within a mile of the western city limits, and some areas near the Bay are designed against liquefaction and subsidence. Soil that moves can break a lateral at one point, and a spot dig and joint repair may be the cleaner answer than lining a pipe that will shift again.

Before anyone digs anything

Anywhere a pit will be opened, underground utilities must be marked first. USA North 811 requires at least two working days of notice before excavation starts, so build that time into the schedule. Gas, electric, water and communication lines can all share the same corridor as the lateral.

What drives the price of a trenchless job

We do not quote prices in a blog post, because the camera footage changes everything. The factors are the length of the lateral, its depth, the number of access pits, the pipe diameter, the repair method and what sits above the pipe. Restoration of paving or landscaping is another real line item.

Hiring someone for this work

Plumbing work that needs a permit is not covered by the California minor-work exemption, which is limited to very small jobs and does not apply when a permit is required. The Contractors State License Board lets you verify a contractor's license, and a C-36 is the plumbing classification. It is worth a two-minute lookup before anyone starts cutting.

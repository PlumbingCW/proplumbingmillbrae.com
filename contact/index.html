For anything that is leaking, backing up, overflowing or not working, call. A phone call lets us ask the right questions, tell you what to shut off and book a visit in the same conversation. Email is slower for all of that, and a wet ceiling does not wait for an inbox.

Call first for anything active

The line is answered 24 hours a day, including nights, weekends and holidays. You do not need to wait for business hours or leave a message and hope. If a pipe has burst or a toilet is overflowing, call before you do anything else, other than closing the main valve.

If you smell gas, do not call us first. Leave the building and call 911 and your gas utility, then call us once you are safe and the gas issue is dealt with.

What to have ready

A few details make the call faster and the visit more useful. None of it has to be perfect, and we will ask for what we need.

  • Your street address and ZIP code, so we know whether you are in Millbrae, Burlingame, San Bruno or South San Francisco.
  • What you see, hear or smell: standing water, a gurgling drain, a stain on the ceiling, no hot water.
  • Where the main water shut-off is, and whether you have already closed it.
  • Whether it is a house, an apartment, a condo or a business, and roughly which floor.
  • The age of the water heater or fixture, if the problem involves one and you can see a label.

When email is the better choice

Use email for things that are not happening right now. That includes a question about a planned remodel, a request to talk through water heater options, or a follow-up on a visit that already happened. Write to the address shown in the contact details below and include your street address and a short description.

Please do not email about an active leak or a backup. Nobody can see an email at 2 a.m. the way they can answer a phone.
